# Zyfiro 10-Day Social Content Calendar

Campaign goal: introduce Zyfiro professionally, explain what the company does, build trust, and invite project inquiries.

Brand tone: polished, direct, calm, premium, practical.

Visual direction for all posters:
- Use Zyfiro logo, dark charcoal or clean white background, restrained accent colors, and modern UI/product mockup elements.
- Keep poster text short. The caption carries the detail.
- Preferred Instagram size: 1080 x 1350.
- Preferred LinkedIn size: 1200 x 1200 or 1200 x 627.
- Add a small footer on each poster: `zyfiro.org | zyfiro@gmail.com`

Recommended posting rhythm:
- Post every 2 to 3 days if quality and engagement matter more than speed.
- Start with brand introduction, then services, process, credibility, values, and invitation.
